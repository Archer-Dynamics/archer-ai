Document ID: HVAC-RX-9104
 Classification: Internal Engineering Reference
 Revision: 7.2

Introduction

This document provides operational, maintenance, compliance, and lifecycle guidance for refrigeration and environmental control assets deployed throughout enterprise facilities.

The HVAC modernization program was created to improve asset visibility, standardize maintenance practices, reduce energy consumption, and improve reliability across multiple facilities.

Prior to modernization efforts, facilities often maintained independent equipment records and inconsistent service practices. This complicated planning activities and reduced visibility into asset condition.

The current program addresses these concerns through centralized standards, structured maintenance programs, and improved documentation requirements.

Asset lifecycle management
