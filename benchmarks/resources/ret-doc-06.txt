Document ID: PSOM-6408
 Classification: Internal Maintenance Reference
 Revision: 5.4

Introduction

This manual provides operational guidance, maintenance standards, inspection procedures, and lifecycle management practices for the pump infrastructure deployed throughout the regional water transfer network.

The pump station program supports multiple facilities and is responsible for maintaining reliable movement of water between treatment systems, storage facilities, and distribution zones.

Historically, maintenance practices varied among sites. Inspection standards, maintenance frequencies, and documentation quality differed substantially between facilities.

A modernization initiative was approved to establish consistent operational expectations and create a unified maintenance framework.

The resulting program introduced standardized inspection procedures, equipment tracking requirements, preventive maintenance schedules, record keeping practices, and reporting standards.

This document forms part of that framework.
