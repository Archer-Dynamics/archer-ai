Meeting Record: Product Steering Committee

The Product Steering Committee met to review priorities for the next development cycle. Participants included representatives from Engineering, Operations, Finance, Customer Support, and Product Management. The meeting focused on balancing feature delivery goals with ongoing platform reliability efforts and support requirements.

Engineering leadership presented status updates for several active development initiatives and reviewed outstanding technical debt items. Operations reported stable infrastructure performance and highlighted recent improvements in deployment automation. Customer Support shared ticket trends indicating that users continue to request enhancements to reporting and dashboard capabilities.

Financial review confirmed that approved project funding remains available and no significant budget concerns were raised. Committee members discussed resource allocation options and evaluated several competing proposals for upcoming work. A risk assessment was completed for each proposal, including implementation complexity, support impact, and expected customer benefit.

After discussion, the committee formally approved Decision ID DEC-7841.

Members agreed that the approved decision should move forward into detailed planning and implementation review. Follow-up actions were assigned to prepare planning materials, define project milestones, and establish success metrics before work begins.

The meeting concluded with agreement that project teams would provide progress updates during the next steering committee session.
