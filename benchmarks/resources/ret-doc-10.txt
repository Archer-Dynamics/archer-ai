Database Platform Governance and Cluster Lifecycle Management Guide

Document ID: DPG-1047
 Classification: Internal Platform Operations
 Revision: 8.0

Introduction

This document provides guidance regarding database platform governance, cluster administration, lifecycle planning, modernization initiatives, operational standards, capacity management, and infrastructure review procedures used throughout the organization.

The database platform supports a wide variety of operational services, customer-facing applications, reporting systems, administrative tools, and internal business processes.

As the environment expanded, leadership identified the need for stronger governance and more consistent operational standards.

The Database Platform Governance Program was subsequently established to improve visibility, reduce operational risk, standardize administration practices, and support long-term planning.

The database governance initiative focuses on several objectives:

Standardized administration
Improved reliability
Stronger lifecycle management
Better capacity forecasting
Reduced operational risk
Consistent documentation
Platform modernization

Progress is reviewed through recurring governance meetings.
